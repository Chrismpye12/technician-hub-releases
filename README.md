## Install

1. On your Android phone, open the download link you were sent and download the file ending in **.apk**.
2. Open the downloaded file. If Android says it can't install apps from this source, tap **Settings**, switch on **Allow from this source**, go back and tap **Install**.
3. Open **Technician HUB** and follow the short setup. Every permission is optional; the app works better with them:
   - **Calendars** so it can find your jobs
   - **Location** for drive time and the Wi-Fi name
   - **Notifications** and **Exact alarms** for leave-by alerts
   - **Microphone** for dictating notes

If Google Play Protect asks about the app, choose **Install anyway** (the app isn't from the Play Store yet).

## Using it

- **Jobs**: your calendar events that match the job keywords. Tap one for details, copy any value with a tap, swipe a field for quick actions.
- **Gold Standard**: the live Shift4 software versions. Copy as text or as a picture.
- **Equipment**: what to load for upcoming jobs and the hardware catalog.
- **Settings**: templates, keywords, links, drive time and more.
- You can also **share notes into the app** from any other app (Share › Technician HUB) to create a job.

## Updates

The app tells you when there's an update and shows what changed (New Features, Improvements, Bug Fixes). Tap **Update Now**. Small updates take seconds. A bigger update downloads a new app file and Android asks you to confirm the install. Nothing you've entered is lost.

You can choose how updates work in **Settings › About & Updates**.

## Send feedback

**Settings › Send Feedback** (or the button on the What's New screen). Say what happened, what you expected, or what you'd like. You can attach a screenshot. It goes straight to the developer.

## Your data

Your jobs stay on your phone. **Settings › Backup & Restore** lets you export a backup file and import it later or on another phone.
